For several years I have been using [Medium](https://medium.com) as the host and
platform for my blog. I don't recall now why I selected Medium in the first
place, but I did like the content I read there as well as how easy it was to
create content. It was also very easy to set up to get paid. That said, it's not
like I was paid a lot for my content. Last year I made $40 all told, and about
half of that was for [one post](Could-I-Possibly-Switch-from-JetBrains-IDEs-to-Neovim.md)!

I can't say if people were turned off or turned away by the paywall. And besides
what Medium's 'partner program' paid, I didn't have anyone [buy me a coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/raysuliteanu).
I did get "claps" --- Medium's version of likes --- and sometimes comments,
though rarely. I also got some followers. I've also been trying to build up my
own "brand" so to speak, with a dedicated website/domain as part of it. I was
also feeling like changing the approach to my content, where anyone could read
and possibly contribute monetarily if they wanted to, rather than having to pay
to read at all and then maybe contributing a little more.

The final reason for moving off of Medium was the technical challenge and opportunity.
Since I'm semi-retired/self-employed/who-knows-what, and I've been teaching myself
new things like React, Typescript, Rust and such, I thought it would be a good
way to expand my knowledge and skills. So I embarked on this journey to create
and migrate my content from Medium to this site. I'm still cross-posting to Medium,
but on Medium the content now says something like "first published on kidoni.dev".

So read on if you're interested in the details.
